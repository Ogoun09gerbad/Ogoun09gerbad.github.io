Research

2026
MSc thesis · AIMS Senegal

From Delta to Policy: Benchmarking Reinforcement Learning against Black–Scholes Delta Hedging under Market Frictions

Ten learned hedging methods (QR-DQN, SAC, TD3, PPO, Deep Hedging) against three classical hedges, with transaction costs and Heston stochastic volatility: 900 training runs and 50,000 test paths per market, with statistical tests corrected for multiple comparisons.

Under examination · code private for now
2026
Research report · MIORPA, University of Oxford

Holographic Krylov Complexity from Gravity

A Routhian reduction makes the holographic momentum–complexity dictionary consistent for charged probes, checked numerically on AdS3 and top-down quiver geometries.

Zenodo (DOI) Code & data
2025
Master 2 thesis · IMSP Benin

Asymptotic Behaviour and Self-Similar Profiles of Solutions to the Heat Equation in ℝN

Solutions converge to a Gaussian self-similar profile as t → ∞, an analogue of the Central Limit Theorem, with convergence rates of order t-1/2, t-1 and beyond under finite-moment conditions. The same mathematics underlies score-based diffusion models.
