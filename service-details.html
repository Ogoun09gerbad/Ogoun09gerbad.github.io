Mathematics Tutoring

With over 2 years of experience as a Mathematics teacher in Benin (Grades 6–11), I offer structured and personalized tutoring sessions in a wide range of mathematics topics.

  • Mathematical analysis: limits, continuity, differentiation, integration
  • Linear algebra: matrices, vector spaces, eigenvalues
  • Probability and statistics: distributions, hypothesis testing
  • Preparation for baccalaureate, university entrance exams

Suitable for: Middle school, high school, and first-year university students.

  • Data visualization with Matplotlib, Seaborn, ggplot2
  • Web scraping and automated data collection
  • High-dimensional data analysis and dimensionality reduction

Machine Learning & AI
