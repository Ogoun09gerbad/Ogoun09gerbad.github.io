Asymptotic Behavior and Self-Similar Profiles of the Heat Equation in RN

This thesis studies the long-time behavior of solutions to the heat equation in RN. We establish convergence towards a self-similar Gaussian profile as t → ∞, drawing a parallel with the Central Limit Theorem. Convergence rates of order t−1/2 and t−1 are derived under finite moment conditions.
