Research Interests & Work

My academic research spans pure mathematical analysis, applied statistics, and modern data science methods.

Partial Differential Equations & Asymptotic Analysis

My Master's thesis focused on the asymptotic behavior of the heat equation in RN. I studied the convergence of solutions towards self-similar Gaussian profiles as time tends to infinity, establishing a formal analogue of the Central Limit Theorem for PDEs. Convergence rates of order t−1/2 and t−1 were derived under finite moment conditions.

Machine Learning & Statistical Modeling

At AIMS Senegal, my current research focuses on applying machine learning and statistical models to real-world problems. I am particularly interested in fraud detection, data mining, and the use of classification algorithms on structured financial datasets. I also explore deep learning methods including CNNs for computer vision tasks.

Climate Dynamics & High-Dimensional Data

I have a growing interest in applying mathematical and statistical methods to climate science and environmental data. This includes high-dimensional data analysis, dimensionality reduction, and developing robust models for complex spatio-temporal datasets.

Graph Theory & Combinatorial Optimization

My earlier work includes research on graph theory and scheduling, modeling real-world optimization problems using combinatorial mathematics. This work demonstrated practical applications of discrete mathematics in operational research and resource management.

AI Club & Applied Projects

As a member of the AI Club at AIMS Senegal, I collaborated on building a smart robot car using embedded ML systems. I also presented at the 2024–2025 general seminar on the topic: "Using ML algorithms for data mining – case study of exam data."

Interested in collaborating on a research project?

I am open to international research collaborations in mathematics, data science, and AI.
